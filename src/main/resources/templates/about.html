Welcome to the FitSquad Hub!

FitSquad Hub brings people together through community workout challenges that make staying active more fun. Choose a fitness quest that fits your schedule, track your workout duration, and earn XP reward points as you make progress.

There is a place for everyone, with difficulty tiers from Beginner to Advanced. Whether you are taking your first step or working toward a new personal best, your squad can help you stay motivated.
